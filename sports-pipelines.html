10K+ rows a weekloaded on daily and weekly schedules, 2 projects delivered end to end

NFL and NBA stats pipelines for StatRankings and DFS Army

Two US sports data companies needed fresh NFL and NBA stats in their databases on a fixed schedule, with no bad rows reaching their sites.

Data Engineer (contract)ByteSquadJul 2025 to Aug 2026

Tap a screenshot to open it full size. StatRankings advanced stats and the DFS Army research station, both fed by the pipelines.

What I built

  • ETL pipelines in Python (Polars) loading into PostgreSQL on AWS RDS, on daily and weekly schedules.
  • Schema checks on every load (NOT NULL, type, unique and CHECK constraints), so bad loads fail before reaching client tables.
  • Slack and email alerts when a run fails.
  • Ran DFS Army's production database: tables, indexes, backups and monitoring.

Hard part

A wrong stat showed up on a client site. I traced it from the front end back to undocumented empty fields in a source API, and rebuilt the stat from a second API.

My role

Solo engineer on both projects, working directly with the US clients from spec to delivery.
