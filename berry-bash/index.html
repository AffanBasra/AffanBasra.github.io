Quick berry mini-games for 2 to 6 players. Most berries wins. Last place gets a dare.

Demo build. 9 mini-games.

How to play

  1. Pick 2 to 6 players. Any seat can be a CPU.
  2. Each round is one short mini-game. Everyone plays the same game.
  3. You earn 1 berry for every player you beat that round.
  4. The last round is worth double.
  5. Most berries at the end wins. Last place gets a dare card.
One device: pass the phone or laptop when the game tells you to.
Online room: everyone opens the game link on their own phone or laptop. One person makes a room, the rest type the code or tap the invite link.
